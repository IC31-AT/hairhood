Privacy Policy

Who we are

Hair Hood, 91B Whiteladies Road, Clifton, Bristol BS8 2NT, is the data controller for this site.

What we collect

Name, phone number, email address and appointment details, submitted when you book. Anything you send us directly is also kept.

Why we use it

To create and manage bookings through Square, our booking and payments provider, to contact you about appointments, and to respond to enquiries.

Legal basis

Performance of contract — fulfilling your booking. Anything beyond that only happens with your consent.

Who we share it with

Square processes and stores booking data on our behalf. Google receives aggregated data for ad performance measurement — see our Cookie Policy. We don't sell your data.

How long we keep it

While you're an active customer, plus 2 years afterwards for record-keeping. Then it's deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or object to how we use your data. Use the contact form on our Contact & FAQ page, or complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We'll update this policy if how we handle your data changes.
